# Agent Onboarding Demo Script

## Goal
Show how an agent turns a raw GDS PNR into a luxury, client-ready PDF in under 60 seconds.

## Setup (30 seconds)
- Open the app.
- Paste a sample PNR with 2 segments (outbound + return).
- Confirm your company name/logo in the branding editor.

## Live Demo (2 minutes)
1. **Paste the PNR**
   - “I’ll paste the GDS PNR exactly as received.”
2. **Generate the itinerary**
   - Click **Generate Ticket**.
   - “We instantly parse passenger and multi‑segment flights.”
3. **Show luxury layout**
   - “Notice the airline‑style grid, clear segment cards, and premium branding.”
4. **Download PDF**
   - Click **Download PDF**.
   - “The PDF matches the on‑screen ticket.”
5. **Explain the gate**
   - “You get up to 5 free PDFs per day — upgrade for unlimited.”

## Common Questions
- **What if the PNR has multiple segments?**  
  “We parse each segment into its own card.”
- **Can I add my agency logo?**  
  “Yes — upload it here, and it appears on the PDF.”
- **What’s included in the free plan?**  
  “Up to 5 PDFs/day — perfect for testing.”

## Optional Close (30 seconds)
“If this saves just 5 minutes per booking, it pays for itself quickly.”
